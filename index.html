What a seettu is

Everyone pays in. One member takes the pool. Then the next.

A seettu is a savings circle between people who trust each other. Each cycle, every member contributes the same amount and one member receives the whole pool. It runs until everyone has had a turn.

Frequency

How it works

From the first invite to the final payout

  1. Start the group

    Set the contribution, the frequency, the number of members and the first due date. Add a late fee if your group uses one.

  2. Invite your circle

    Share the group code over WhatsApp or SMS. People ask to join, and the organiser approves each request.

  3. Pay and show the slip

    Members pay by bank transfer and upload the slip, or hand over cash for the organiser to mark. Part payments are counted too.

  4. Hand over the pool

    The organiser records the payout. The member whose turn it is confirms they received it, and the next cycle begins.

Features

Built around how groups really run

Payments that match real life

Not everyone pays the full amount on the same day, or the same way. The ledger keeps up.

  • Part payments. Pay in pieces and see exactly what is left.
  • Slips for transfers. Upload the bank slip and the group can view it.
  • Cash mode. The organiser marks cash as received, and the ledger says so.
  • Late fees. Set once in the group rules and added automatically.

A payout order nobody can argue with

Choose how turns are decided when you create the group. Whatever you pick, the result is saved for everyone to see.

  • Fixed order. The organiser sets the line and members can see it from day one.
  • Lottery draw. Run the draw in the app. Each draw gets a record number and is saved permanently.
  • Bidding (Wendesi). Members bid for an earlier turn. The bid comes out of their payout and is shared with the group.
  • Turn swaps. Two members can agree to trade turns, with the organiser's approval.

Know who you are saving with

A seettu only works when people keep their word. Seettu Circle shows each member's record, so trust is built on what has happened.

  • Trust score. Every member's on-time payment history, cycle by cycle.
  • NIC verification. Verified members carry a badge. The group never sees the NIC number or the photos.
  • Guarantors. Groups can require an existing member to vouch for each new person.
  • Join requests. Nobody enters the group until the organiser approves them.

Nobody misses a due date

Reminders reach people even when their phone silences app notifications, and there is a fair way to ask for time.

  • SMS backup. If the morning app alert goes unopened, a text message follows.
  • Grace periods. In an emergency, a member can ask for up to 7 more days with no late fee. The group votes within 24 hours.
  • Group chat. Payments, payouts and votes are posted automatically, alongside your messages.
  • Needs your attention. One list on the home screen of what is waiting on you.

Questions

Before you bring your group

Does Seettu Circle hold our money?

No. Members pay each other the way they already do, by bank transfer or cash. The app records each payment, keeps the slip and shows the ledger to the whole group.

How do I sign in?

With your mobile number. We send a 6-digit code by SMS, and there is no password to remember.

Who can see my NIC?

Group members only see the Verified badge. They never see your NIC number or your photos.

How is the payout order decided?

Your group chooses one of three ways: a fixed order set by the organiser, a lottery draw run in the app, or bidding. Members can also request to swap turns.

What happens if someone cannot pay on time?

They can pay part now and the rest later, or ask the group for a grace period of up to 7 days. If your group set a late fee, it applies to overdue payments that have no approved grace period.

Can I be in more than one seettu?

Yes. You can create or join several groups and switch between them from the home screen.
